About the Fashion Archive

✨

A digital exploration of American fashion history from the 1900s to the present day, documenting how clothing and style have both reflected and influenced social, political, and cultural movements throughout the decades.

🎯

Project Mission

Fashion Archive aims to document and analyze the evolution of American fashion throughout the 20th and 21st centuries. By examining fashion through a historical lens, I hope to provide context for how the clothes we wear connect to broader societal shifts and personal expression.

Each decade tells a unique story about American identity, values, and aspirations. This project seeks to preserve these stories and make them accessible to students, educators, fashion enthusiasts, and anyone curious about the intersection of fashion and history.

The website was coded entirely by hand, combining my interests in fashion history and STEM. This project represents my passion for both fashion history and web development, showcasing how these seemingly different fields can come together to create something educational and engaging.

🔍

Research Methodology

I conducted thorough research to ensure the accuracy and depth of the information presented. This project draws from multiple sources to create a comprehensive view of American fashion evolution:

📰

Primary Sources

Fashion magazines, advertisements, photographs, and personal accounts from each era provide authentic perspectives on how fashion was presented and perceived in its time.

📚

Academic Resources

Books and articles on fashion history, cultural studies, and American history help contextualize fashion trends within broader historical movements.

🏛️

Museum Collections

Virtual visits to fashion collections at institutions like The Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Fashion Institute of Technology provide insights into historical garments.

🧩

Cultural Analysis

Analysis of how fashion intersects with social movements, economic conditions, technological advancements, and artistic expressions creates a multidimensional understanding of each era's style.

About the Creator

👩‍💻 High School Student

My name is Daniel Lee, and I'm a high school student with a passion for exploring the intersection of fashion, technology, and cultural studies. This project was created as part of my school's Genius Hour program, where I was given 1 hour per week to work on something I'm passionate about.

I decided to merge my interest in fashion history with STEM by coding this website completely from scratch. This allowed me to develop both my research skills and my technical abilities, creating something that represents both sides of my interests.

My fascination with fashion history began when I discovered how clothing serves as a tangible artifact of social history. I believe that by studying what people wore, we can better understand how they lived, what they valued, and how they saw themselves.

💡 Genius Hour Project

This website represents my solo Genius Hour project, where I dedicated 1 hour per week to researching fashion history and teaching myself web development. Every aspect of this site—from the research to the design to the code—was created entirely by me, showcasing how passion projects can help high school students develop valuable skills while exploring their interests.
